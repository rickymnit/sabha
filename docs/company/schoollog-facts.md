## 3. Modules

### Fee management [schoollog.in/modules/school-fee-management-system]
- Predicts collection risk, sends automated reminders before, on and after due dates with the amount and a payment link, and gives a daily collection forecast.
- Parents pay by UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, including straight from a WhatsApp link. Receipts are instant and reconciliation is automatic.
- Fee heads by class, category or programme. Instalments, due dates and automatic late-fee rules.
- Concessions: sibling, staff-ward, scholarship. Optional transport, hostel and activity fees.
- Partial payments and payment plans. Bulk payment links to defaulters by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
- Works with "major payment gateways" that deposit into the school's bank account. No named gateway is stated.
- When a student leaves mid-year, future invoices stop and the history is kept.
- Tally integration is mentioned [schoollog.in/platform, brochure]. Fee collection "Online fees, defaulters, receipts" [nav].

### Student attendance [schoollog.in/modules/student-attendance-management-system]
- Methods: face and camera (existing webcams or USB cameras), biometric (fingerprint or iris terminals), mobile app (works offline), RFID card, bus attendance.
- "Mark a class of 40 in 30 seconds". Parents get an SMS and app alert when a child is marked absent.
- Flags attendance dips (example: 95% to 80%) and builds a school-specific at-risk model.
- Teachers can edit within 24 hours. After that, an admin and a reason are needed.
- Statuses: present, absent, on leave, late arrival, early departure. Thresholds can differ by class (example: Nursery 90%, seniors 75%).
- Tracks shifts and campuses. Records are kept indefinitely. Staff attendance is included. Reports export to Excel.

### Biometric attendance [schoollog.in/modules/school-biometric-attendance-system]
- Fingerprint: under 1 second, 99.9% accuracy, ₹2,000–₹8,000 per device.
- Face recognition: 1–2 seconds, 98–99% accuracy, ₹5,000–₹15,000 per device.
- RFID card: under 0.5 seconds, "100% scan", ₹50–₹200 per card.
- Classroom camera: one photo marks a class.
- "3 days" to go live and "15 min" staff training.
- Devices connect by Wi-Fi or USB. Works with existing devices through an API. Brands can be mixed.
- Hardware is a one-time cost, with "no per-scan or per-device monthly charge".
- Offline queue with duplicate detection. Parents or staff can ask for biometric data to be deleted. Templates are encrypted and not stored as raw images.
- Staff attendance feeds payroll.

### Bus attendance and transport [schoollog.in/modules/bus-attendance-system]
- Boarding and alighting are marked by RFID card or conductor app ("under a second"). The AI compares expected riders with actual riders and flags missing ones before the bus leaves.
- Live GPS. GPS is "typically accurate to within 5–10 meters in open areas".
- AI-predicted arrival times "within five minutes" (hero: "±5 min ETA accuracy"). Missed pickup flagged in "< 60 sec". "100% Rides audit-logged".
- Parents are notified at boarding, school arrival, return boarding and drop-off. They can track in a browser link or the app.
- Fleet, routes and stops, AI route optimisation. Vehicle documents: permits, insurance, pollution certificates. Drivers and attendants. Transport fees linked to fee collection.
- Multiple or outsourced fleets. Records kept indefinitely. 4 steps to go live.

### Examination management [schoollog.in/modules/examination-management-system]
- Datesheets, rooms and invigilators. Marks entry by browser or mobile, with live validation.
- Grading set to the board. Report cards for a whole class in one action. Results go to the app and WhatsApp.
- Promotion and result processing.
- AI question paper with answer key in about 60 seconds (class, chapters, marks and difficulty). Early-warning flags.
- 800+ report-card formats across CBSE, ICSE, State boards, IB and IGCSE, plus the NEP 2020 HPC. "Format not in there? We build it during onboarding."
- Admit cards [nav] and seating plans [schoollog.in/school-erp].
- Platform page: the question-paper generator works "from a large question bank" [schoollog.in/platform].

### Holistic Progress Card (HPC) [schoollog.in/modules/holistic-progress-card]
- NEP 2020-aligned. 360-degree input from self, peer, teacher and parent assessment.
- Stages: Foundational (Classes 1–2), Preparatory (3–5), Middle (6–8), Secondary (9–12).
- AI drafts descriptive remarks. A whole class is generated in one action, and the teacher reviews before publishing.
- "800+ report card and HPC formats across CBSE, ICSE, State boards and IB". It can match the school's existing format.
- Launched Jul 2024 [schoollog.in/team].

### Admission management [schoollog.in/modules/school-admission-management-system]
- Enquiries come from the website, WhatsApp, admin entry and bulk import. Automated follow-up sequences. AI lead scoring.
- AI document checks (type, validity, legibility). Shortlisting on the school's criteria. Source attribution.
- Online application portal with parent status tracking. Interview scheduling.
- Admission and deposit fees by cards, net banking, UPI and wallets.
- Admitted students move automatically into the ERP.
- Different criteria per class or board. Referral tracking. Export to Excel.
- Scales "from 50 to 5,000 applications in a season". Hero claim: "Setup in under 30 minutes".
- Demo offer: "30-minute tailored walkthrough", migration plan, "Pricing for your student count".
- Brochure also lists Pre-Admission CRM, Lead Management, Campaign Management and Digital Marketing [brochure].

### Student enrollment [schoollog.in/modules/student-enrollment-management-system]
- Starts after the offer is accepted: student record with a unique admission number, documents (birth certificate, previous report card, TC, photographs, Aadhaar copy), and section allotment (capacity, gender balance, sibling rules).
- Assigns the fee plan, transport and hostel. Sends parent and student logins on WhatsApp and SMS.
- Bulk enrollment from admissions or Excel. Promotion uses results and the school's rules.
- Transfers and withdrawals include a dues and clearance check, then a TC is generated.
- AI: reads documents to fill the record, chases missing documents on WhatsApp, balances sections, projects next session's strength.
- Compliance: UDISE+ student data, APAAR ID, PEN, board registration and LOC (CBSE registration for Classes 9 and 11, LOC for 10 and 12), class-wise strength, category and RTE reports.
- When a student leaves, the record is closed, not deleted.

### Student database [schoollog.in/modules/school-database-management-system]
- One student record shared by admissions, academics, attendance, fees, transport and documents. Includes medical history and a document vault.
- Migration: data audit, AI cleaning, validation, gradual go-live. Imports Excel, CSV and other ERPs.
- Role-based access with an immutable audit trail. Parents can view but not edit.
- Full export to Excel or CSV. Scales to "10,000 or more students". Graduated students are kept with a graduated status.
- Can integrate with existing systems through APIs. "Most schools are fully operational within a week of launch."
- Data on encrypted servers in India. Backups are "Automatic and continuous".

### Inventory [schoollog.in/modules/school-inventory-management-system]
- Covers uniforms and textbooks (by size, class, set), stationery, lab and sports equipment, furniture and IT assets (warranty, repair, handover).
- School store sells to parents. Purchases can be added to the fee bill.
- Items are issued to named students or staff, with due dates. Damage and loss are recovered through fees.
- Indents, approval chains, quote comparison, purchase orders, goods received notes, vendor records.
- AI: reorder alerts with a draft PO, demand forecast from enrolment, stock-audit reconciliation.
- Reports export to Excel or PDF. Multi-campus with stock transfers. Canteen and store are listed [brochure].

### Administration (HR, payroll, front office) [schoollog.in/modules/school-administration-system]
- Staff profiles. Leave with approval chains and automatic substitution suggestions.
- Payroll from biometric or app attendance and leave. PF and ESI handled. Digital payslips.
- Front office: visitor log, gate passes, enquiries, call log.
- Certificates (transfer, bonafide, character, fee) generated in seconds, numbered and logged.
- AI drafts circulars, notices and letters (approved before sending). Plain-English or Hindi questions.
- Multi-campus, including staff transfers between campuses. Reports export to Excel or PDF.
- The "20+ hours lost each week" figure is labelled "Illustrative" on the page.

### School mobile app [schoollog.in/modules/school-mobile-app]
- iOS and Android. Role views for management, teachers, parents and students. "Everyone downloads the same app."
- One parent account covers multiple children.
- White-label options (school logo, colours, name). Offline access to timetables, grades and attendance.
- AI progress writer from lesson notes. Camera attendance. Hindi, English and voice.
- Families can do everything on WhatsApp instead.

### WhatsApp for education [schoollog.in/modules/whatsapp-for-education]
- Runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) from the school's own verified number. Sends attendance alerts, fee reminders with a payment link, circulars, homework, exam schedules and report cards (as a secure PDF).
- Two-way: replies are routed to the right staff member, absence replies become leave, PTM replies book a slot.
- AI drafts messages (approved before sending). Hindi and English, including Hindi typed in English letters.
- Opt-in consent is recorded. Templates are approved by Meta. Message logs are stored in India. Delivery and read tracking.
- SMS fallback for parents without WhatsApp.
- The school needs a number that is not already on WhatsApp. Meta's message charges are extra and shown in the quote.
- Go-live time depends on Meta's verification and template approval.
