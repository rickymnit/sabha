## School management system FAQs

What is a school management system used for?
A school management system is used to run everything a school does administratively: admissions and student records, daily attendance, timetables, exams and report cards, fee collection and accounts, staff payroll, transport tracking and parent communication. It replaces separate registers, spreadsheets and standalone apps with one place where data is entered once and reused everywhere.
How much does a school management system cost in India?
School management software in India is usually priced per student per year, and the total depends on your student count, the modules you switch on and how many campuses you run. Schoollog is quoted per school after a short requirement-mapping call, so you pay only for the modules your school actually uses.
Is Schoollog cloud-based?
Yes. Schoollog is a cloud-based school management system, so there is no server to buy or maintain at the school. Staff use it in a browser and parents, students and teachers use the iOS and Android apps. Your data stays in India, encrypted at rest and in transit and isolated per school.
Does Schoollog support CBSE, ICSE and State boards?
Yes. Schoollog supports CBSE, ICSE, State, IB and IGCSE, configured first-hand rather than bolted on. That includes board-specific report card formats, grading scales and compliance reporting, with 800+ report card formats already built in.
How long does it take to implement Schoollog?
Schoollog handles migration, configuration and training for you rather than leaving it to your staff. Your historical data, report card formats, school structure, academic configuration and workflows all move across, so switching from an existing system does not mean rebuilding your school.
Can Schoollog manage multiple campuses?
Yes. 40+ school groups run every campus from one console. Group leadership can see attendance, academics, fees, staff, performance and alerts across every campus without asking each campus for a report.
What makes Schoollog different from a traditional school ERP?
A traditional school ERP records what happened and waits for someone to run a report. Schoollog acts on what it records: attendance marked by a teacher updates the student record, notifies the parent, recalculates risk indicators, changes the Principal's dashboard and creates a follow-up, without anyone opening a second screen.
Do parents need to install an app to use Schoollog?
No. Parents can use the Schoollog app on iOS and Android, or stay entirely on WhatsApp. Attendance updates, fee reminders, circulars and report cards reach them either way, and they can reply, request and pay without installing anything.

## What is a School Bus Attendance System?
A school bus attendance system tracks which students board and alight from school buses, records movement through the transport route, notifies parents of arrival and delays, and optimizes bus routes using real data.
Instead of paper registers and guesswork, Schoollog gives schools real-time visibility into who is on each bus, where it is, and when it will reach every stop, with AI watching every route for the gaps a human register would only reveal hours later.

## School Bus Attendance System FAQs

Contact Us
What if a student misses their bus in the morning?
The system flags it to the school immediately. The coordinator can call the family, confirm the student is safe, and record the absence before the route continues.
How do we handle students picked up by parents?
The conductor marks them as not boarded due to parent pickup. School attendance still shows them as present, while bus attendance accurately records that they did not use transport.
Can we change routes mid-year?
Yes. You can redesign routes as student distribution or traffic patterns change, test the new plan, notify families, and retain all historical data.
What if a bus breaks down?
The driver can report the breakdown, alert management, reassign students or arrange backup transport, and automatically share the delay and updated ETA with parents.
How far back can we access bus attendance records?
Indefinitely. Schoollog stores attendance history for reporting, long-term pattern analysis, and compliance.
Can we track chronic no-shows on buses?
Yes. Attendance patterns per student help schools investigate alternative transport, repeated absence, and possible dropout risk.
Do parents need special equipment to see tracking?
No. Any internet-connected phone works. Parents can open a tracking link in their browser or use the Schoollog app.
What if we have multiple or outsourced bus fleets?
Schoollog tracks each operator independently while consolidating every bus into one management view.
Can bus fees integrate with overall fee collection?
Yes. Transport dues appear in the student's overall fee status, and schools can contact families about pending transport fees in bulk.
How accurate is the GPS tracking?
GPS is typically accurate to within 5–10 meters in open areas and remains precise enough in dense urban areas to show parents which street the bus is on.
What happens if a student is on the wrong bus?
The system compares the boarded bus with the student's assigned bus and alerts the coordinator so the mismatch can be resolved quickly.

## Answers for the way real school days run
Enrollment, devices, privacy, and offline capture, explained so you can decide with confidence.
Contact Us
How is biometric enrollment done?
Enrollment is a one-time capture. During admission or staff onboarding, a fingerprint or face record is taken at the gate device or office terminal in a few seconds. Compatible records from an existing biometric system can also be imported, so re-enrollment is not forced on schools that already have data.
How long does enrollment take per student or staff member?
A single fingerprint or face capture typically takes under five seconds per person. A whole school can be enrolled over a day or two, depending on strength, by running enrollment stations at key entry points.
What if a student cannot enroll biometrically?
Every student always has a reliable way to check in. If a fingerprint does not scan, the student falls back to face recognition, an RFID card, or manual marking. Most students scan successfully on the first try.
Can a parent opt out or request deletion of biometric data?
Yes. Parents can request permanent deletion of their child's biometric record, and staff can request the same for their own records. Schoollog removes the encrypted template on request and continues marking attendance through a non-biometric method.
Will it work with our existing biometric devices?
Yes. Schoollog can integrate with existing biometric devices through an API, so schools can upgrade gradually instead of replacing working hardware all at once.
What types of devices are supported?
Fingerprint scanners, face recognition cameras, RFID card readers, and classroom cameras are all supported. Devices connect over Wi-Fi or USB and can be assigned to gates, classrooms, or offices with their own operating times.
Are we locked into a specific brand of hardware?
No. Hardware is a one-time investment and there is no per-scan or per-device monthly charge. Schools can mix brands and add more locations as they grow, without being tied to one vendor.
Can biometric data be stolen or misused?
Biometric data is encrypted end to end, stored in secure vaults, and restricted to authorized personnel. Templates are not stored as raw images, and access is logged for audit.
Where is the data stored, and who can access it?
Encrypted templates are held in Schoollog's secure storage with role-based access. Only authorized school staff can view identifying records, and every access is recorded in an audit trail.
Do you sell or share biometric data with third parties?
No. Biometric data is used only to mark and reconcile attendance for the school. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing or any unrelated purpose.
Can attendance records be exported and audited?
Yes. Full history, approved leave, duplicate detection, and exportable reports keep records defensible for inspections and audits.
What happens to attendance when the internet connection drops?
Devices queue records locally and sync automatically when connectivity returns. No attendance is lost during an outage.
How long can a device keep capturing offline?
Devices store verified timestamps locally and continue marking attendance for the duration of an outage. Once the connection returns, the queue flushes to Schoollog in order, with no manual export needed.
Will offline records create duplicates when they sync later?
No. Duplicate detection reconciles offline and online scans, so the same check-in is not counted twice even if a student taps twice or records arrive out of order.
Does offline capture still send parent alerts?
Alerts are queued alongside attendance and delivered as soon as sync completes, so parents still receive arrival and absence notifications after a brief network gap.

Can we keep our existing system and integrate with Schoollog?

What if we add new students mid-term?
New students can be enrolled at any time. Their class, academic, attendance and fee records become available immediately without a manual sync.
Can students’ data be edited by parents?
No. Parents can view their own child’s information, but only authorized school staff can modify student records.
Can we export the entire student database to Excel?

What if a student’s name uses a non-English script?

Are there limits on how much data a student record can hold?

What happens to a student’s data after they graduate?

Can we track information changes over time?

## School inventory management FAQs
Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to walk you through this module.
Contact Us
What is a school inventory management system used for?
It tracks everything the school buys, stores, sells and lends, from uniforms and books to lab equipment, furniture and IT devices, so stock, money and responsibility always match.
Can parents buy uniforms and books online?
Yes. Parents see the items and book sets for their child's class, choose sizes, and either pay online or add the amount to the fee bill. Orders are packed and ready to collect.
Can store purchases be added to the fee bill?
Yes. Store purchases, damages and lost items can be added to the student's fee account and paid with the next instalment, with one receipt.
How do we track items issued to students and staff?
Every issue is recorded against a named student, staff member, class or department, with a due date. Returns are checked in, and overdue or damaged items are flagged.
Does Schoollog manage purchase orders and vendors?
Yes. Departments raise indents, approvals follow your chain, quotes are compared and purchase orders are sent to vendors. Goods received are checked against the order and stock updates automatically.
Can we track furniture and IT assets?
Yes. Furniture and devices are recorded as assets with a tag, location or assigned person, condition, warranty and repair history, and appear in the asset register.
How does AI help with inventory?
It warns you before stock runs low and drafts purchase orders, predicts next session's book and uniform demand from enrolment, and reconciles stock audits against recorded movements.
Who can see and change stock data?
Access is role based. Store staff record sales and issues, department heads raise and approve indents, accountants see purchases and fee billing, and the principal sees reports. Each user sees only what their role allows, and every change is logged with the user's name.
What inventory reports can we generate?
Stock on hand, fast and slow moving items, daily store sales, items issued and overdue, purchase orders pending, vendor wise spending, asset register and audit variance. Reports can be filtered by campus, category or date range and exported to Excel or PDF.
Can we run a store for more than one campus?
Yes. Each campus keeps its own stock and store, while leadership sees stock, sales and purchases across every campus, and stock can be transferred between campuses.

## Attendance management system FAQs
Straight answers for school leaders planning the move from registers to real-time attendance.
Contact Us
Can we still use paper registers as a backup?
Yes, but we don't recommend duplicate paper and digital systems because they create extra work and data conflicts. A better backup is a weekly Excel export.
What if a student is marked absent by mistake?
Teachers can edit attendance within 24 hours from the mobile app or web portal. Every change records its time and author. After 24 hours, an admin and a reason are required.
Do parents need an app to see attendance?
No. Parents receive real-time SMS alerts and can also use the parent portal to see attendance history, trends, and absence reasons.
Can different classes have different thresholds?
Absolutely. Nursery could require 90% while senior classes require 75%. Schoollog tracks and flags each class against its own rule.
How does Schoollog's AI learn what 'at risk' means?
It learns from your school's historical patterns (which low-attendance students later dropped out and which warning signs appeared first) to build a school-specific risk model.
Can we track late arrivals separately?
Yes. Schoollog distinguishes present, absent, on leave, late arrival, and early departure. You can set different rules for every status.
What if we have multiple shifts or campuses?
Attendance is tracked separately by shift, class, and campus, with location dashboards and a consolidated whole-school view.
Is biometric attendance mandatory?
No. Mixed methods are common: face recognition for primary classes, the mobile app for seniors, and conductor-marked bus attendance, all consolidated in one view.
How far back can we access records?
Indefinitely. Schoollog stores historical attendance so reports and long-term trends remain available across years.
What happens if the internet drops?
Mobile attendance works offline. Teachers keep marking on their phone and the data syncs when the connection returns. Web-based marking requires a connection.

## Student enrollment management system FAQs

What is the difference between admission and enrollment?
Admission is the process of selecting a student, from enquiry to offer. Enrollment starts when the offer is accepted and covers creating the student record, documents, section, fees, transport and accounts, and every promotion and transfer after that.
Can we enroll new students in bulk?
Yes. You can import new students from the admissions module or an Excel sheet, then allot sections, assign fee plans and routes and send app logins to a whole class in one action.
How does student promotion work in Schoollog?
Schoollog uses final results and your promotion rules to move students to the next class and section together. Exceptions such as detentions or pending results are flagged for review before you confirm.
Can parents upload documents online?
Yes. Parents upload documents from their phone through a secure link, and the office verifies each one. Families with missing documents get automatic reminders.
How are transfer certificates generated?
When a withdrawal is approved and dues and clearances are checked, Schoollog generates the TC from existing student data in your school's format, numbers it and keeps a copy in the student's record.
Does Schoollog help with UDISE+ and APAAR ID?
Yes. Student records hold the fields needed for UDISE+ and APAAR ID, and Schoollog shows which records are incomplete so you can fix them before the deadline.
Can a student move between sections or campuses mid-session?
Yes. Section, stream and campus transfers update the timetable, teachers, attendance, fees and transport together, and the full history stays on the student's record.
What happens to a student's data after they leave?
The record is closed, not deleted. Academic history, documents, fee history and the TC stay available for verification and audit, with role-based access.

Everyone's phone number is visible.

Nobody controls it.

Is it legal for schools to message parents on WhatsApp?
Yes, when parents have agreed to receive messages and the school uses the WhatsApp Business Platform as intended. Schoollog records each parent's consent, sends alerts only through approved templates and keeps a log of every message, which also helps the school meet its duties under the DPDP Act.
Do parents need to install an app?
No. Parents receive updates, reply and pay fees on WhatsApp, which they already use. Parents who want more detail, such as full marks history or a homework timeline, can also use the Schoollog app.
Can we use our school's existing phone number?
You can use a number the school owns, as long as it is not currently active on the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. Many schools use a new dedicated number so the office landline or a staff phone is not tied up.
How is this different from class WhatsApp groups?
Messages are one-to-one and personal, parents cannot see each other's numbers, teachers do not share their personal numbers, and every message and reply is recorded with the school. Important notices are not buried in group chatter.
Can parents reply to messages?
Yes. Replies are two-way. Routine questions are answered from school records, absence explanations are recorded as leave, and anything that needs a person goes to the right staff member.
What does WhatsApp messaging cost?
Meta charges for WhatsApp Business Platform messages based on the type of message, and these rates change from time to time. Your Schoollog quote shows the messaging cost for your school's expected volume, so there are no surprises.
What happens if a parent doesn't use WhatsApp?
The same update is sent by SMS instead, and parents who use the Schoollog app see it there too. Every family is covered.
Can messages be sent in Hindi?
Yes. Each family can receive messages in Hindi or English, and Schoollog understands replies in either language, including Hindi typed in English letters.
Can teachers message parents without sharing their personal numbers?

Free vs paid
## Free school management software: what to know
Free school management software exists in three forms: open-source software you host yourself, free plans with limits on students or features, and free trials of paid products. Each can work, but the costs usually move somewhere else:
Open source
You host it yourself, and you own the upkeep.
Free plans
Limited students or modules, with add-ons billed separately.
Free trials
The full paid product, for a limited period.
- Open-source software needs a server, someone to install and update it, and someone to fix it when it breaks.
- Free plans often cap students, lock key modules or charge separately for messaging and support.
- Switching later means migrating every record a second time, usually in the middle of a session.
- Data protection still applies. A free tool that stores children's data carelessly creates the same obligations under the DPDP Act as a paid one.
A free plan can make sense for a very small school that wants to test the idea of going digital. For a school that runs admissions, fees and exams every year, the useful comparison is total cost over three years, not the price in year one.
